Chinese Brand Name Strategy Helper

Foreign brands that skip a Chinese name lose customers and get the name squatted. Plan yours before entering China.

Pick a direction

  • Transliteration (音译): match the sound — e.g. Nuvora → 诺维拉 / 诺维朗. Choose characters with a positive meaning.
  • Semantic (意译): keep the meaning — e.g. SunBrew → 日酿 / 阳光酿. Risk: may be descriptive and weak.
  • Hybrid: sound + meaning — the most memorable (e.g. Coca-Cola → 可口可乐).

Check before you settle

  • Does the chosen combination sound bad in any common Chinese dialect?
  • Does it mean something unintended or embarrassing?
  • Is the pinyin already registered by someone else in your classes?
  • Will Chinese customers pronounce and search it easily?
This lists the planning steps; a real name must be cleared against the CNIPA database and registered. Not legal advice.
