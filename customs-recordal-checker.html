China Customs IP Recordal — Eligibility & Materials Checker

海关知识产权保护备案 — have China Customs automatically detain counterfeits at the border (imports and exports). Recordal is free, valid 10 years and takes about a month to set up. Answer four questions to see if your right qualifies and what documents you must prepare.
What recordal does: once your trademark / copyright / patent is recorded with the General Administration of Customs (GACC), customs officers can detain suspected infringing goods at Chinese ports and notify you — for both goods made in China being exported and fakes being imported. The record is valid 10 years, renewable, and free of official charge (Regulations on Customs Protection of IP Rights, Art. 7; GACC IP portal).
