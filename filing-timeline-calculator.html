Critical deadlines

These two deadlines are easy to miss from overseas:
• Refusal review (驳回复审): only 15 days from receiving the CNIPA refusal — a Chinese agent must respond in Chinese.
• Opposition (异议): only 3 months from preliminary publication; after that you must use invalidation instead.
