Key facts & official sources

ItemDetail
Assignment feeCNY 450 per class (e-filing) / CNY 500 per class (paper) — per trademark
License recordal feeCNY 135 per class (e-filing) / CNY 150 per class (paper)
Effective dateAn assignment takes effect only from the date CNIPA registers it (Trademark Law Art. 42) — the buyer is not the owner until then
Same-mark ruleThe assignor must transfer identical or similar marks registered for the same goods in one go (Art. 42) — selling one mark while keeping a similar one is not allowed
Licence filingLicensing a mark should be filed with CNIPA for the record (Art. 43); until recorded, it is less effective against third parties
Foreign partiesForeign companies / individuals must act through a CNIPA-recorded Chinese agency and provide a China service address
