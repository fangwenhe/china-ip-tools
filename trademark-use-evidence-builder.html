The three things every item must show

  • The trademark — the mark as registered, used in an identical or visually indistinguishable form (a materially different logo may not count).
  • The registered goods/services — use on the exact items (or closely related items) listed in the registration; use on other goods does not save the mark.
  • Real, open commercial use within the relevant period — dated, and falling inside the three years before a cancellation filing. Single, isolated or internal-only use is usually insufficient.
