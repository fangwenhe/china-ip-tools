China Copyright Registration — Materials Checklist

National copyright registration (作品著作权 / 软著) in China has no official government fee, but the materials and format must be right or the application bounces. Pick your work type and tick what you have.

Typical timing: 30–60 working days via an agent. Official fee: none for the certificate; you pay only service/translation costs. Registration is prima-facie evidence in disputes.
Checklist for planning; exact requirements follow the National Copyright Administration (CPCC). Not legal advice.
