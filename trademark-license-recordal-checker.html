Standing to act in infringement proceedings follows the licence type above; state the type clearly in the contract, because ambiguity weakens both sides.

Key facts & official sources

ItemDetail
Legal basisTrademark Law Art. 43 and Implementing Regulations Art. 69: the licensor files the recordal during the contract term; recordal materials must state licensor, licensee, licence period and licensed goods/services scope.
Recordal feeCNY 135 per class online / CNY 150 per class on paper (Fee for Recording Trademark Licensing Agreement), per mark.
One form ruleOne recordal form covers one registered mark licensed to one licensee. One contract licensing several marks to the same licensee needs one contract copy but a separate form per mark.
Term limitThe licence period must stay within the remaining validity of the registration; it cannot extend past the registration's 10-year term.
Effect against third partiesThe contract is valid between the parties even if unrecorded, but an unrecorded licence cannot be relied on against a good-faith third party (e.g. an earlier licensee or a later buyer of the mark). Record promptly after signing.
Foreign licensorA foreign owner without a China domicile or premises must act through a CNIPA-recorded Chinese agency and provide a China service address; a China-based licensee is not a substitute.
Regulated goodsFor human medicine or tobacco, the licensee must also attach its industry approval / production or sales permit.
Sources: CNIPA Guidance on Trademark Licence Recordal Procedure · CNIPA official fees (EN) · WIPO Lex: Measures for the Recordation of Trademark License Contracts. Official fees are current as of November 2026; confirm at filing time.
