Grounds CNIPA refuses or later cancels a pledge

GroundRule
Owner mismatchPledgor's name does not match the CNIPA record and no proof shows it is the registered owner
Illegal contractThe contract violates a mandatory provision of law
Mark no longer validThe mark has been revoked, cancelled, invalidated, or expired without renewal
Court seal / freezeThe mark has been sealed or frozen by a people's court
Co-owner consent missingA jointly-owned mark is pledged without the other co-owners' consent (unless agreed otherwise)
Later cancellationRegistration is withdrawn if a refusal ground surfaces, the pledge contract is void/rescinded, the mark loses rights through legal proceedings, or false documents were used
Non-compliant filings get a 30-day correction window; missing the window or an unsatisfactory correction means the application is treated as withdrawn.

Key facts & official sources

ItemDetail
Legal basisTrademark Law Implementing Regulations Art. 70; CNIPA Announcement No. 358, Measures for the Procedures of Pledge Registration of Registered Trademark Exclusive Rights, effective 1 May 2020.
Who appliesThe application is filed jointly by pledgor and pledgee, directly or through a trademark agency. A foreign person/entity without a habitual residence or premises in China must appoint an agency.
Official feeCNIPA charges no government fee for pledge registration. Agent fees, valuation and legal costs are separate and are not CNIPA charges.
When the pledge arisesThe pledge right is established on the registration date. CNIPA issues the Trademark Pledge Registration Certificate to both parties within 2 working days of acceptance.
Identical/similar marksThe pledgor must also pledge identical or similar marks registered on the same or similar goods/services; registration numbers must be stated in the contract and application.
Later proceduresSeparate filings handle a name change or secured-amount change, an extension of the pledge term, cancellation after repayment, and reissue of a lost certificate. The pledge lapses automatically when its term ends.
Disposing of a pledged markTransferring or licensing a pledged mark generally requires the pledgee's consent; record this restriction in the pledge contract and confirm with the pledgee before any deal.
Sources: CNIPA Announcement No. 358 — Pledge Registration Procedures · State Council Gazette (English translation reference). Rules are current as of November 2026; confirm with CNIPA or your agent at filing time.
