China Trademark Priority Claim — 6-Month Deadline Calculator

If you have already filed the same mark in your home country (or another Paris Convention country), you can claim that earlier filing date in China — but only within 6 months, and you must declare the priority at the time of the China filing. Enter your first filing date below.
Two traps that wipe out your priority:
  • Not declaring priority on the China application form. You cannot add it later. If the CNIPA form is filed without the priority declaration, the filing date becomes your China date — even if you are still inside the 6-month window.
  • Not submitting the certified copy of your home-country filing within 3 months. China requires an official certified copy (from the home office, apostilled or legalised) within 3 months of the China filing date. Missing this is treated as if you never claimed priority.

When priority is worth claiming: if you have even one day of earlier home filing, declare it — it costs nothing extra on the CNIPA side (the 270 RMB official fee is unchanged), and it is your strongest shield against a Chinese squatter who filed after your home date but before your China date.
