Key facts & official basis

ItemDetail
First-to-fileChina grants rights to the first valid application; prior overseas use, marketing or Amazon sales do not create priority rights in China. The registered owner controls enforcement, licensing and assignment.
Foreign-party agency ruleUnder Trademark Law Art. 11 (2026 revision; formerly Art. 18), a foreign company or individual without a habitual residence or place of business in China must entrust a CNIPA-recorded trademark agency. A mainland WFOE is a Chinese entity, not treated as the foreign company's China place of business.
Agent / representative squattingArt. 22 (formerly Art. 15): an agent or representative may not register the principal's mark in its own name without authorization; a party with a contract or business relationship who knowingly files a prior-used mark is likewise refused. Interested parties may oppose or seek invalidation.
Assignment recordalTransfer to another entity: CNY 450 per class online (CNY 500 paper), per mark. The new owner becomes entitled only when CNIPA registers the assignment (Art. 42); the assignor must also transfer similar marks on the same goods.
Licence recordalRecord a licence between owner and user: CNY 135 per class online (CNY 150 paper). Until recorded, the licence is weaker against third parties.
Name / address changeRecord an owner rename or address change: free on e-filing, CNY 150 per class on paper; all marks of the entity should be updated together.
Sources: Trademark Law of the PRC (2026 revision, CNIPA) · CNIPA trademark application FAQ guide · CNIPA fee guide. Rules and fees current as of November 2026; confirm at filing time.
