Non-Use Cancellation (撤三) Risk Checker

Any third party can cancel a registered China trademark that has not been genuinely used for 3 consecutive years. Check whether your mark is in the exposure window.
Pick a registration date.

If exposed, keep this use evidence (使用证据)

  • Sales invoices, receipts and e-commerce order records showing the mark on real goods
  • Packaging, labels, tags with the mark affixed
  • Advertising, shows, distribution contracts — with dates and the mark visible
  • Use must be in China, on the registered goods/services, within the last 3 years
Rough indicator only. "Use" is interpreted strictly by CNIPA; nominal/token use may be disregarded. Not legal advice.
