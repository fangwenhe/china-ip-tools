What does my China trademark status mean?

Pick the status shown on your CNIPA record (商标网 / Trademark Office database). This decoder explains what the stage means, the typical waiting time, your next action and any strict deadline. It does not replace the gazette entry or your agent's case file.

The smooth case, step by step

1. Filing & formal examination

CNIPA checks applicant qualification, documents, Nice list, POA and fee. About one month in a smooth case. Completing formalities establishes the filing date.

2. Substantive examination

Examiner reviews distinctiveness, prior identical/similar marks and prohibited elements. Statutory ceiling: nine months from receipt; typical four to six months in a smooth case. CNIPA does not issue a search report.

3. Preliminary approval & publication

Approved marks are published in the Trademark Gazette. Third parties may oppose: three months for publications before 1 January 2027; two months under the 2026 revised Trademark Law for publications on or after that date.

4. Registration

If no opposition is filed, or the opposition fails, the mark registers and a certificate issues. The exclusive right lasts ten years, renewable indefinitely.

Frequently asked

What does “实质审查中” (under substantive examination) mean?

The examiner is checking whether your mark can register: distinctiveness, conflict with earlier identical/similar marks on identical or similar goods, and any prohibited elements. This stage does not yet mean approval — a refusal may still issue. CNIPA does not provide a search report.

How long does substantive examination take in China?

The statutory ceiling is nine months from receipt of the application (Trademark Law Art. 32). In a smooth case preliminary approval usually arrives around four to six months after filing; refusals, oppositions or backlog push the date later.

What should I do after a rejection (驳回通知)?

You have 15 calendar days from receipt to request a CNIPA review (复审). The review decision issues within nine months (extendable three); if it goes against you, you may sue the Beijing IP Court within 30 days. Act through a CNIPA-recorded agent; foreign holders cannot file directly.

What does preliminary approval publication (初步审定公告) mean?

It means examination passed and the mark was published for opposition. The registration has not issued yet. The opposition window is three months (publications before 1 Jan 2027) or two months (publications on/after 1 Jan 2027). If no opposition succeeds, the mark registers at the end of the window.

My status says “异议中” — how long until a decision?

CNIPA must decide the opposition within 12 months after the opposition window ends, extendable by six months in special cases. Oppositions typically add many months to the registration timeline, so respond promptly through your agent.
